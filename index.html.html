Important advisory disclaimer

Pathfinder Admission Consultants provides strictly advisory counselling services. All preference lists and institute suggestions are prepared solely on the basis of the candidate's declared JEE Main 2026 and/or JEE Advanced 2026 rank, category, and stated preferences, cross-referenced with historical JOSAA/CSAB closing rank data. These suggestions do not guarantee seat allotment, admission, or selection to any institute or branch. Actual seat allotment is determined exclusively by the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) and the Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB). The final responsibility for locking choices on the official portal rests entirely with the candidate and/or their guardian. All fees paid are strictly non-refundable once registration is complete, regardless of allotment outcome or candidate withdrawal.
